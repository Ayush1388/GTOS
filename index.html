    How does this work?

    None of these apps has a link that sends a file for you, so the page converts the clip in your browser. WhatsApp and Telegram get a looping GIF (up to 6 s). Instagram gets a silent video (short clips are looped to at least 3 s, up to 15 s), because its chats only animate GIFs from its own library. On phones the send button opens the share sheet; on desktop it downloads the file.
