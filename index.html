GIF → WhatsApp sticker

Paste a Twitter / X link that has a GIF or video.

How does this work?

WhatsApp has no link that sends a sticker or GIF directly, so the page converts the clip in your browser, then opens your phone's share sheet. Sticker = 512×512 animated WebP (under 500 KB, up to 6 s). GIF = silent looping MP4 (up to 6 s), which WhatsApp plays as a GIF. Pick WhatsApp and a chat. On desktop, download the file and drag it into a WhatsApp chat or use a sticker-pack app.
