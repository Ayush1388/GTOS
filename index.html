Turn any tweet GIF into a WhatsApp sticker

Paste a Twitter / X link. Get a sticker or GIF ready to send.

How does this work?

WhatsApp has no link that sends a sticker or GIF directly, so the page converts the clip in your browser. Sticker is a 512×512 animated WebP (under 500 KB, up to 6 s) that you add with WhatsApp's Attach → Sticker. GIF is a silent looping MP4 (up to 6 s) that WhatsApp plays as a GIF; on phones the button opens the share sheet.
